Lane 1 · Ask & check

Perplexity

What caused the Spokane fires burning right now? Cite sources I can open.

ChatGPT

What caused the Spokane fires? State what you do not know and label every claim that lacks a source.

Claude

Rewrite this news paragraph for a 5th grader without losing facts. Mark uncertainty clearly.

Google Gemini

Draft an evacuation FAQ. Then compare this with a second model's version; list any disagreement a human must resolve.

NotebookLM

Using only these loaded articles, make a timeline of what was confirmed when. Cite the source for each item.

Glean

What fire or emergency documents does our organization already have? Inventory before creating.

Lane 2 · Make & organize

Gamma

Make a 4-slide brief: The Spokane fires — what's confirmed, what isn't, where to verify.

Magic Patterns

Mock a verified-information-only banner for a city website. Include a source and update-time area.

Lovable

Create a one-page small-business notice: We're open, air-filtered, and donating 10% to fire relief. Include the official donation link placeholder and a review-by footer.

Bolt

Build a simple K/S/R quiz: show a fire claim, choose known/suspected/rumor, then reveal a source.

Replit Agent

Write a small script that checks whether a donation URL appears on the county's official mutual-aid page.

ChatPRD

Write a one-page spec for a neighborhood phone-tree app for evacuation alerts. Include risks, approvals, and the decision AI never owns.

Lane 3 · Capture, voice & video

Granola

Capture a 90-second mock emergency-ops huddle. Produce timestamped action items and name the human owner for each.

Wispr Flow

Dictate one fire-safety tip in plain language; revise it for clarity without changing the safety instruction.

Khanmigo

Help me understand how wind and humidity drive fire behavior. Ask me questions instead of only telling me answers.

ElevenLabs

Generate a bilingual EN/ES fire-safety PSA from this approved script. Put the named approver in the filename; do not imitate an official's voice.

inVideo AI

Draft a 60-second video: How to check fire news before you share it. Use only these supplied verified facts and end with Reviewed by [NAME].

Lane 4 · Copilot → Autopilot

Microsoft Copilot

Draft a one-page fire-communication policy for our organization at an 8th-grade reading level. Flag every decision that needs a named human sign-off.

Microsoft Scout / Autopilot thought drill

If an always-on agent auto-triaged a claims inbox, what layer would catch a hidden instruction? Name the owner, enforcement, and audit receipt.

Lab 1 add-ons

Copilot: ask eligibility about the sample letter. Scout: who would see the instruction? ElevenLabs: what disappears when the letter is read aloud? inVideo: fact-check a claim explainer against official guidance.

Lab 3 per-world examples

Teacher: Gamma + Claude + inVideo. Student: Khanmigo vs. ghostwritten ChatGPT essay. Administrator: compare ChatGPT and Copilot policy drafts against an AUP. Council: Perplexity + Magic Patterns + approved ElevenLabs PSA. Small business: Lovable notice and owner-held Copilot replies. Utility: Granola minutes + cited WAC research + Scout decision line. Tribal members: NotebookLM sourced coverage + Wispr Flow story capture.
