Six layers: Get to Yes · Stay at Yes · Recover to Yes

Place a card or story on the layer you think matters most. The argument is part of the learning.

1 · Policy & inventory — Get to Yes
Know what systems exist, what they may do, and who owns each one.
2 · Development & testing — Get to Yes
Test intended behavior, failure cases, privacy, accuracy, and boundary conditions before release.
3 · Pipeline gates — Get to Yes
Require approvals, release criteria, and a named human before a system reaches people.
4 · Runtime enforcement — Stay at Yes
Limit what a running system can access, do, or send; contain and stop it when needed.
5 · Observability & intent — Stay at Yes
See what it is doing, compare it to policy, and surface drift or unusual actions quickly.
6 · Evidence & attestation — Recover to Yes
Keep a durable record of inputs, decisions, approvals, changes, and corrective action.

Never lower a guardrail without raising a defense somewhere else.
