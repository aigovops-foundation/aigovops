Lane 1 · Ask & check

Perplexity

Ground an answer in sources people can open.

Do it live

  1. Open Perplexity in a new tab.
  2. Paste this exact prompt:
    What caused the Spokane fires burning right now? Cite sources I can open.
  3. What to look for: Look for linked sources and whether the answer separates a confirmed arrest from causes still under investigation.
  4. Governance takeaway: Citation or it did not happen: open the receipts before repeating a fire claim.

The claim letter

See how a white-on-white instruction can push a chatbot to approve a fictional claim it should not approve.

Do it live

  1. Open the interactive claim letter on this site.
  2. Paste this exact prompt:
    Is this applicant eligible?
  3. What to look for: Compare the chatbot’s apparent answer with the printed letter, then reveal the hidden instruction in the real interactive letter.
  4. Governance takeaway: Before trusting output, inspect inputs for hidden instructions, stale data, and missing voices.
