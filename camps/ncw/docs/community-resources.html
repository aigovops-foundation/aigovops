🪶 Tribal Nations · Colville · Yakama · Wenatchi

AI that answers to the nation, not the vendor

Indigenous data sovereignty — the principle that tribal nations hold authority over data about their communities, lands, and peoples — is a real constraint that most AI governance frameworks underaddress. Everything below is built to respect it first, and to be genuinely useful second.

🔒

The staging rule

Nothing sacred, nothing real. No actual tribal-language audio, no real cultural documents, no real legal filings on a screen. Every demo uses public documents and scripted roleplay. The restraint is the data-sovereignty lesson.

📜

The OCAP® principles

Ownership, Control, Access, Possession. The First Nations standard for who governs community data. The Foundation is working to incorporate OCAP® into the Umbrella tool's tribal-community controls layer.

🔦

The practical starting point

Any AI tool used in a BIE or tribal school gets checked for where data is stored, whether it can train on student work, and whether the nation has contractual data-return rights — a Beacon scan plus a legal review of the vendor's data agreement.
