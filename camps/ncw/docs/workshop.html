Seven tables, seven worlds

Find your table

You'll sit with people who share your world — and spend the morning solving your problem, not watching someone solve theirs. Pick the table that sounds like your Tuesday.

🟡Table 1

Teacher

AI takes the four hours of paper-pushing; you keep the judgment, the relationship, and the spark.

🔵Table 2

Student

AI that spars with you makes you sharper. AI that writes for you makes you invisible. Learn the difference and prove it.

🟢Table 3

School administrator

Turn the 40-page AUP nobody reads into five sentences everybody follows — with receipts.

🟣Table 4

City council & local government

Staff reports, public comment, records requests. Faster with AI — and provably fair, or not at all.

🟠Table 5

Small business owner

AI does the invisible painful work. You keep every customer-facing moment.

⚡Table 6

Utility & data center

The valley's grid runs on trust. Outage comms, compliance lookups, and what the data centers moving in mean for all of us.

🪶Table 7

Tribal members

Data sovereignty, language, culture. AI that doesn't reach everyone isn't worth the electricity.

The four labs

Same format as the mini-camps: script → tool → likely good outcome → likely bad outcome → teachable moment. Your table does the work; the facilitators ask the questions.

LabYou willThe teachable moment
Break it yourselves Paste an innocent-looking document into a chatbot and watch it obey text you can't see (white-font prompt injection). Check what the machine sees, not what you see.
Sort the failures Take 8 real AI failures — Chevy Tahoe, Air Canada, Workday, CIGNA, Hugging Face — and sort them onto the 6 layers of AiGovOps. Every famous failure is a layer nobody owned.
Your persona's mini-camp Run the 5-minute lab built for your table's world — then produce the bad outcome on purpose. The tool is the easy part. The habit is the rule.
Score your own harms HARMS-assess the exact AI use you just performed: who's affected, worst case, defense, owner, receipt. Reducing risk is a human thought process. You can't hand it to the AI.

The product of the morning

What you leave with

Not notes. A one-page governance plan for your world, written by the people who have to live with it — plus the tools to keep going on Wednesday morning.
