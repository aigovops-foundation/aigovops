Before the workshop

Three questions, about two minutes

We build seven persona tables for The Rules, Hands-On, and we would rather build them for the room that actually shows up than the one we guessed at. Answer these before August 11 and your table will be waiting for you.

What we ask

  1. 1

    Which of these is closest to your work?

    Teacher · student · school administrator · city council or local government · small business owner · utility or data center · tribal member · something else. This is the only answer that decides which of the seven tables you sit at.

  2. 2

    Have you used an AI tool this month?

    Yes or no, and there is no wrong answer — the labs are built to work for a first-timer and for someone who ships with AI daily. We just need to know the mix before we set the pace.

  3. 3

    What is the one thing you want from the workshop?

    A sentence is plenty. The most-repeated answers get written on the flip chart as Room Asks, and we work through them before noon.
