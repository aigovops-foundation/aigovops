## How to read the columns

- **Sent** — the date it actually left an outbox or a form got submitted, not the date it was drafted.
- **Replied** — any human response, including a decline. A decline is a finished row, not a failure.
- **Committed $** — what they said yes to in writing. **Paid** — what cleared Stripe.
- **Accounts granted** — the two 12-month educational accounts, when they're in hand.
- **Logo live** — the logo is up on `docs/supporters.html` and staged for the event screen.
- **Notes** — pre-filled only where `sponsor-emails.md` already flags something. ⚠️ = the only
  published address is a legal/privacy/support inbox, so consider LinkedIn or their contact form in
  parallel.

Empty cell = hasn't happened yet. Don't pre-fill a "no" — leave it blank until someone tells you.
