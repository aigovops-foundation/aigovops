> **v3:** owns the **audit gate** · built on **VH-013 Robodebt**, **VH-003 Toeslagenaffaire** and **VH-088 Post Office Horizon** · maps to the **AAL-4 Cryptographic Attestation** pattern · mark by two 400s or a founder. **Ongoing. Verify offline, claim honestly, contribute upstream, host.**
Status: **decide** — this kit describes what ships in P2 (days 61–90) if PRD v3 is approved.

## Who arrives here

A 300 who is ready to steward: to be verified by strangers, to verify strangers, and to teach the next
cohort. This is the level the ladder exists to produce. Within ninety days a 400 hosts a Thursday; within
a year most Thursdays are hosted by 400s, and the founders keep the two jobs the redesign left them.

## What you practise

| Part | What you do | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Declare** | Contribute a UCID crosswalk or a policy to Umbrella through the RFC process, with a rationale and a migration note (v1 §13) | Umbrella; the RFC process |

| **Read** | Write or improve a lens: the words a regulator, an auditor, a compliance lead or an engineer sees. Teach a 100 to read theirs | Lantern |
| **Return** | Host a Thursday. Land a contributor's PR. Mark a 300's step. Last line: *You are on the host rota. The next Thursday is yours.* | the Community |

## Take-home

A public verification statement with your name on it, and a steward role. The statement is about a
**system** — *OVERT Level 1, gate coverage 96% over 30 days, verified by <your name>* — in the exact
sentence form from v1 §6, so a procurement reviewer can check it.

## The return

You host. A host's mark on another practitioner's level is itself a receipt (`practice.mark`, with you
as approver — the same hold-and-release shape as a C3, applied to people). The community teaches itself.

## What must be true before this kit is called shipped (PRD v2 §8, P2)

- Offline verifier released; evidence bundle export with framework crosswalk
- The GitHub Action (Lantern v0.3); the RFC process open
- The Foundation publishes its own OVERT level and gate coverage honestly
- **Exit test:** one third party verifies a bundle offline and publishes that they did; one 400 hosts a Thursday

## What this level is not

Not a system certification, not an exam, not sold. It *is* a practitioner credential: marks made by hosts
on Thursday, minted as receipts on the ledger, free to attempt (PRD v3 D10 and the §11 exclusion list).
