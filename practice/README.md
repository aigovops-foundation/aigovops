# Open-Source-Unified-Sept-2026

**One practice, four levels, three tools, one community.**
AiGovOps Foundation · Programme: Labor-Day-Big-Redesign · Started 11 September 2026 · Status: **decide**

This is the working project for unifying the Foundation's three open-source projects — Umbrella, Beacon
and Lantern — around the **practitioner**, not the architecture. Nothing here is live and no code in the
three repos has changed. When approved, the public name proposed for this repo is
`aigovops-foundation/practice` (see [Decision 7](docs/DECISIONS.md)).

## The idea in three sentences

Each practitioner starts where they are. Each level practises all the parts, not one part. The tools
bring you back, because the community is where you become a practitioner — the tools only get you started.

## The ladder

| Level | Name | You arrive as… | You leave with… | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [**100**](levels/100-begin/) | Begin | anyone — nothing installed | one signed receipt bundle, and a Thursday to bring it to | 10 min |
| [**200**](levels/200-retrofit/) | Retrofit | a 100 with an agent of your own | your policy, a receipt chain, a diff — and your story | 30 min |
| [**300**](levels/300-hold/) | Hold | a 200 whose agent touches people or money | a coverage claim you can defend; someone else's bundle verified | a week of traffic |
| [**400**](levels/400-prove/) | Prove it to a stranger | a 300 ready to steward | a public verification with your name on it; a Thursday you host | ongoing |

Every level practises the same five parts — **declare · decide · prove · read · return** — at increasing
depth, with the same three tools throughout. No level adds a new tool name. Levels grade *practice*, never
systems: a system's assurance is its OVERT level and its gate coverage number, and those never appear in
the same sentence as a practitioner's level.
