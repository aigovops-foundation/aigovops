## The rule

> No direct model-to-tool path for consequential actions.

The marks are the credential, and the credential is a receipt. A level describes a *person's*
practice; a system's assurance is its OVERT AAL and its gate coverage number — the two sentences never merge.

*Get to yes. Stay at yes. Recover to yes. Prove it in ten minutes. Then bring it, and be counted.*
