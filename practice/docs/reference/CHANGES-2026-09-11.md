One day, one project, one release candidate, twenty-two decisions. This is the record for Ken, for the
brain, and for the first stranger who asks "why is it like this?". Everything below is on GitHub; the
site is the door; the decisions are on [the Decision Sheet](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/1f2aa96e-144f-4025-880a-098348a6e759).

## The through-line

The Foundation's open-source projects were three downloads and a decision about which to install first.
By the end of the day they are one practice with four levels — 100 Begin, 200 Retrofit, 300 Hold,
400 Prove — each an hour or less, each on a real case from the 100 verified harms, each running the four
gates (pre-pend · policy · operate · audit), each ending in something a person can show on Thursday. The
marks are the credential, and the credential is a receipt. The hero of the story is the standard — OVERT,
from Glacis — and the Foundation is the community that carries it to everyone the market will not reach.
