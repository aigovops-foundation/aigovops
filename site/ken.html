Ken Johnston

AI & Data Leader · CEO, Autonomic.ai · Former Microsoft & Ford

Ken Johnston brings decades of experience in AI, data strategy, and product leadership from some of the world's most recognized technology and manufacturing companies. As CEO of Autonomic.ai, he led the development of Ford's connected vehicle data platform — one of the largest IoT data infrastructure deployments in the automotive industry.

At Microsoft, Ken held senior roles spanning product engineering, data platform, and AI strategy, working across Azure and enterprise products. His career bridges the gap between cutting-edge AI capability and the operational, regulatory, and governance realities that enterprises face when deploying AI at scale.

Ken co-founded the AiGovOps Foundation with Bob Rapp to build the open-source tooling and community that practitioners need to make AI governance as rigorous and verifiable as production engineering — starting with Beacon, Lantern, and the OVERT 1.0 protocol.
