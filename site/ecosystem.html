The four properties and their functions

The library

aigovops-library (the governed core)
The source of truth — and the running v1 core. Defines the agents, skills, and design every other property uses.

Functions

  • 13 governed agents (Concierge, Lantern, Beacon, Maker, Scribe, Cloud-Mary…)
  • Skill catalog (framework-map, beacon-sign-evidence, accessibility-audit…)
