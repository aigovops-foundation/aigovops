Short (50 words)

The AiGovOps Foundation is a 501(c)(3) open-source nonprofit that builds AI governance tooling for practitioners. Its flagship products — Beacon, Umbrella, and Lantern — implement the OVERT 1.0 cryptographic evidence standard, turning regulatory compliance into testable, versioned code under an Apache-2.0 licence.

Long (100 words)

The AiGovOps Foundation is a 501(c)(3) open-source nonprofit co-founded by Ken Johnston (CEO of Autonomic.ai, former Microsoft and Ford) and Bob Rapp (formerly Vodafone, IBM Watson, GE Healthcare, Microsoft, and General Motors). The Foundation builds free, verifiable AI governance tooling under Apache-2.0: Beacon discovers and signs AI evidence bundles cryptographically; Umbrella compiles governance policy into executable YAML controls; Lantern reads Beacon's signed bundles into human-readable reports. All implement OVERT 1.0, the Foundation's open cryptographic evidence standard. Coverage spans 40+ global frameworks including the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, GDPR, and HIPAA. The Foundation's mission: make AI governance as rigorous and auditable as production engineering.
