Beacon, Umbrella, and Vendor RFI each have a CONTRIBUTING.md on main; Lantern has none.

Are there open good-first-issue tasks?

Starter tasks will be labelled “good first issue” on GitHub; until then, start from a project's contribution guide.

The Thursday meeting is 15:00–16:00 Pacific.
