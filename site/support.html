Common questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes — the AiGovOps Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations via Open Collective are tax-deductible in the United States. Check your local laws for international deductibility.

What payment methods are accepted?

GitHub Sponsors accepts credit cards and PayPal — activate the foundation's Sponsors profile at github.com/sponsors. Open Collective accepts credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and crypto. Both handle recurring and one-time donations.

Can my company be invoiced?

Yes — Open Collective supports invoicing for corporate contributions. Select "Organization" when contributing and download your invoice for accounting.

What if I want to contribute code instead?

Even better! Check the open issues on Beacon and Lantern. First-time contributors get the same credit as financial supporters.

How do I get listed as a Sustainer?

Once your contribution is confirmed on either platform, email bob.rapp@aigovops.community with your name/logo and we'll add you within 48 hours.

Is there a monthly option?

Yes — both GitHub Sponsors and Open Collective support monthly recurring donations. You can cancel anytime with no penalty. Monthly sustainers keep the infrastructure running reliably.
