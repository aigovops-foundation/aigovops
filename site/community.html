The practice — four levels, one hour each

Whatever your policy skill or your technical skill, start where you are. Every level runs the four gates — before an action, before a rule ships, while it runs, and after — on a real case from the 100 verified harms, and ends with something you can show on Thursday. Built on OVERT, the open standard from Glacis Technologies.
