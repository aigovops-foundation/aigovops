The gate law. Every action is a proposal, and the gate answers in exactly one of three ways: 1 yes — earned, with evidence, run once on a single-use receipt; 0 no — refused, and written to the ledger; ? held for a human — and decays to 0 if no one approves by the deadline.

Support

  • Is my donation tax-deductible? Yes — the AiGovOps Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Donations are tax-deductible in the United States. Check your local laws for international deductibility.
  • What payment methods are accepted? We use Stripe for secure checkout — it accepts all major credit and debit cards, and handles both one-time and recurring monthly donations. Card details go straight to Stripe (PCI-compliant); the foundation never sees or stores them.
  • Can my company be invoiced? Yes — need an invoice for a corporate contribution? Stripe emails a receipt automatically, and for a formal invoice email bob.rapp@aigovops.community and we'll provide one.
  • What if I want to contribute code instead? Even better! Check the open issues on Beacon and Umbrella. First-time contributors get the same credit as financial supporters.
  • How do I get listed as a Sustainer? Once your contribution is confirmed on either platform, email bob.rapp@aigovops.community with your name/logo and we'll add you within 48 hours.
  • Is there a monthly option? Yes — Stripe supports monthly recurring donations. You can cancel anytime with no penalty. Monthly sustainers keep the infrastructure running reliably.
