Bob Rapp

AI/ML Leader · Vodafone · IBM Watson · GE Healthcare · Microsoft · General Motors

Bob Rapp has spent his career at the intersection of enterprise AI, data engineering, and real-world deployment — across industries where getting AI wrong has real consequences. From healthcare diagnostics at GE to connected vehicles at General Motors, from Watson enterprise solutions at IBM to global data infrastructure at Vodafone, Bob has seen firsthand what happens when governance is an afterthought.

That experience is what drives his work on the AiGovOps Foundation. He believes the tools exist to make AI governance as disciplined and verifiable as software engineering — but only if they're open, practical, and built by practitioners for practitioners. Not consultants, not vendors, not committees.

Bob co-founded the Foundation with Ken Johnston and leads the development of Beacon (the AI scanner and evidence signer), the OVERT 1.0 protocol, and the community of engineers and compliance professionals who are making policy-as-code a reality in production environments.
