🧪 Alpha demo · end to end

Watch governance run like engineering.

Two AiGovOps alpha projects, one continuous story — from a governed agent platform that puts every action through a gate, to an open vendor RFI that turns “which tools?” into a signed decision. Every step is on the record.

Act 1 · the platform that governs

Every action is a proposal. The gate decides.

Omni runs a fleet of 30 agents with no ambient authority — the only way any of them touches the world is core.mediator.perform, and the gate answers in exactly one of three ways:

1

Yes — earned, with evidence. Runs once, on a single-use receipt.

0

No — refused, and written to the ledger.

?

Held for a human — and decays to 0 if no one approves by the deadline.

Do real work from your phone — every send still gated

/draft Q3 governance brief
✅ draft_post — done. (verdict 1 · earned)
/publish draft-12
⏳ publish_post held for approval.
ApproveDeny
✅ published — receipt signed, used once. (you tapped Approve)

Holds fan out to your channels; an approval performs exactly once. The Jeeves Fleet cockpit shows the whole fleet at a glance — what’s pending, what’s wired, and what each agent is allowed to do — and leads with what needs you.

Act 2 · then decide, openly

From “which vendors?” to a signed decision.

The open-source Vendor RFI turns AI-governance tool selection into the same evidence-first move: compose a stack, compare the field, approve a shortlist, and leave with a receipt — not a sales deck.

Compose a stack → Compare 50 vendors → Approve a shortlist → Decision receipt
