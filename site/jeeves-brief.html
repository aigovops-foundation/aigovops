The site runs itself. Humans hold the keys.

A multi-agent operation where everything reversible happens automatically — and every irreversible move pauses for a named human. One-page brief.

A master agent, Jeeves, takes a plain-English goal, plans it, and dispatches a fleet of departments to do the work. A cryptographic gate sits on every action: reversible work proceeds on its own; anything irreversible holds for a steward. The result is a platform that runs ~99% on its own, with a human in the loop exactly — and only — where it counts.

The one law

1Proceed. The effect is reversible (a draft, a read, an un-publish). No human needed.
?Hold for a steward. The effect is irreversible — send, pay, deploy, delete. Waits for Ken or Bob.
0Stop. Unsafe or unreviewed. It does not run.

Agents have no ambient authority — every effect goes through the gate, and every one that runs is Ed25519-signed into an auditable ledger.
