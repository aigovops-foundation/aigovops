What is AiGovOps?

AiGovOps is the practice of treating AI governance the way mature engineering teams treat code: versioned, tested, and verifiable before deployment. The AiGovOps Foundation exists to build the open tooling and standards that make this practical for any organization. Its operating description is concise: policy as code, evidence that ships, and governance an auditor can verify with one command.

Key facts

  • AiGovOps is the practice of treating AI governance the way mature engineering teams treat code.
  • The Foundation builds open tooling and standards to make this practical for any organization.
  • The three engines are Beacon signs, Umbrella declares, and Lantern reads.
  • The Foundation is co-steward of OVERT with CHAI.
  • The front door offers six actions: become a member, give, join a meeting, read the newsletter, get an audit plan, or book the founders.
