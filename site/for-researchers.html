Frequently asked questions

What does OVERT specify?

OVERT is an open standard for independently verifiable runtime evidence that declared policies, security controls, and oversight actions executed, without exporting protected content.

What is in the Library?

The Library contains 100 verified AI-harm cases mapped to NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and OWASP.

Can I search the Library immediately?
