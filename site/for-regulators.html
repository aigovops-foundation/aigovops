Frequently asked questions

How can a deployer prove what an AI system did?

OVERT is an open standard for independently verifiable runtime evidence of declared policies, security controls, and oversight actions.

How can I verify a receipt?

Beacon’s public browser demo creates a key in front of you and provides a bundle an auditor can check with VERIFY.

Who co-stewards OVERT with the Foundation?

The Foundation co-stewards OVERT with CHAI (Coalition for Health AI).
