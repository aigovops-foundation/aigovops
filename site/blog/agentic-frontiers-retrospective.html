Agentic Frontiers: A Two-Month Retrospective on Global AI Governance (Jan–Mar 2026)

Bob Rapp ·April 2026 ·7 min read

The first quarter of 2026 marked a definitive turning point in the evolution of artificial intelligence. We have officially moved beyond the era of "AI as a tool" — simple chatbots and creative assistants — into the era of "AI as an employee."

This shift toward Agentic AI — systems capable of planning, executing, and iterating on complex tasks autonomously — has forced a rapid maturation of global governance frameworks.

This retrospective reviews the most consequential regulatory and standards developments from January through March 2026, and what they signal for organizations deploying autonomous agents.
