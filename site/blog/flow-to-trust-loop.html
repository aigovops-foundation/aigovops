The Flow-to-Trust Loop: How 10 Ops Disciplines Converge to Ship Trusted AI

Ken Johnston ·March 2026 ·12 min read

A practitioner's guide to the modern AI Ops stack. Shipping trusted AI isn't the job of a single team or a single tool — it's the convergence of ten operational disciplines, from DataOps and MLOps through SecOps, GovOps, and beyond.

Each discipline has matured on its own track, with its own tooling and culture. The Flow-to-Trust Loop is the model for how they connect: how flow (the speed of shipping) and trust (the confidence that what shipped is safe and compliant) reinforce rather than fight each other.

This long-form piece maps all ten disciplines, shows where they hand off to one another, and explains how to align governance with deployment pipelines so that trust becomes a property of the system rather than a gate bolted on at the end.
