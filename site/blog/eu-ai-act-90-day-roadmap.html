EU AI Act Ready by August 2026? Here's Your 90-Day Compliance Roadmap

Bob Rapp ·March 2026 ·6 min read

The clock is ticking. August 2, 2026 marks the applicability date for key EU AI Act obligations covering high-risk AI systems under Annex III. If your organization operates high-risk AI systems in the European market, the window to prepare is closing fast.

The challenge: many organizations still lack practical clarity on what Article 6 classification actually requires, and how to produce the conformity assessment evidence regulators will expect.

This roadmap breaks the work into a practical 90-day sequence — classification, gap assessment, technical documentation, and evidence generation — so high-risk operators can move from uncertainty to a defensible compliance position.
