What is AiGovOps?

AiGovOps is governance, risk, and compliance for AI — implemented as code, telemetry, and operating practice. We move past intent statements to verifiable outcomes: signed receipts, audit trails, lifecycle controls, and human oversight you can demonstrate.

  • Define harm before deployment
  • Make governance observable
  • Turn policy into evidence
  • Ship good AI. Prevent bad AI.

Four pillars

Audit

Inventory every model and agent. Sign what matters. Keep evidence.

Oversight

Human-in-the-loop where it counts. Kill switches that actually work.

Ethics as code

Ethical frameworks expressed as policy, tests, and CI checks.

Compliance

Map to NIST, ISO, EU AI Act. Hand auditors a bundle, not a binder.
