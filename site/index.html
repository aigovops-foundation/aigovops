The Open-Source Toolkit

Three tools. One verifiable evidence chain.

Beacon signs the evidence. Umbrella compiles it into executable controls. Lantern reads it back as a human-readable report. All Apache-2.0. All under the AiGovOps Foundation.

🔦 Scanner + Signer

AiGovOps Beacon

Finds every AI on your network. Attests them against 23 audit frameworks. Produces a cryptographically signed bundle any auditor can re-verify with one command. OVERT 1.0 Protocol.

See it sign — live →
🏮 Compiler

AiGovOps Umbrella

Converts 40+ global frameworks — EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, Korea AI Basic Act — into executable YAML controls you can run in CI.
