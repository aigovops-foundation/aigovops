**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-06-03

---

## 1. Market context

The global AI governance market is expanding at pace with regulatory activity. The EU AI Act entered force in 2024, NIST AI RMF has become a de-facto US standard, and 40+ jurisdictions have enacted or proposed AI-specific regulations as of 2026. Organizations deploying AI face a fragmented, expensive landscape of closed, vendor-locked compliance tooling.

**The gap:** No open-source, Apache-2.0 toolchain exists that covers the full governance cycle — scanning AI systems, signing cryptographic evidence, compiling policy as code, and producing human-readable reports. The AiGovOps Foundation fills this gap with Beacon, Lantern, and OVERT 1.0.
