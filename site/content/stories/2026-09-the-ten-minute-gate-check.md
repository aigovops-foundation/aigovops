We built the AiGovOps community around one loop: learn a little, try it, produce evidence,
gain confidence. The first pass through that loop should take ten minutes and leave you
holding something.

So here is the first thing you can hold: **[the Gate Check](https://community.aigovops-foundation.com/gate-check.html)**.

## What it is

Ten questions about an AI agent you are being asked to say yes or no to. Each one is a
yes or a no, and each one names the evidence you would point at — a scope statement with an
owner's name on it, the log you opened yourself this week, the name of the person who last
turned it off. There is no "maybe" on the page. A maybe is a no with homework.

At the end you get a Gate Card: the verdict and the exact gaps, in plain text you can paste
into a ticket, a review, or a Slack thread. Copy it, download it, print it. It runs entirely
in your browser; nothing you answer is sent anywhere.

## Who it is for

The manager who has to decide this quarter and has nobody to ask. The lead who is being
shown a demo on Thursday. The reviewer who will be asked, after something goes wrong, why
they said yes.

## What comes next

A second check is already there — [is this vendor's AI claim checkable?](https://community.aigovops-foundation.com/gate-check.html?c=vendor-claim) —
and one more arrives each month. Inside the community, your cards accumulate on your profile,
and the deep version (a signed evidence bundle from Beacon) is where the ten-minute check
points when you are ready.

Take one home. Then tell us what was wrong with it — the thumbs at the bottom of the page go
straight to Ken and me.
