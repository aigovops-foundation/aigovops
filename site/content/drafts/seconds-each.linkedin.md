Seconds each — the hold that Cigna's PXDX never had

In 2023 ProPublica reported that Cigna used an automated review process called PXDX that let medical directors deny large batches of claims in seconds each, often without opening the patient file. Class actions followed in California. Cigna denies that the algorithm decided anything. And California passed SB 1120, which restricts utilization-management denials made by an algorithm alone. This is VH-035 in the Foundation's hundred verified AI harms. Notice what the law had to say out loud: a person must actually review. Not "a person is in the loop." A signature on a batch is a person in the loop. The question level 300 asks is the one the regulators ended up asking — out of how many? The corpus writes it: a human medical reviewer actually reviews before any claim is denied; batch denials without records review are off the table.
