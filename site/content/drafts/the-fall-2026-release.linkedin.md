The Fall 2026 Release — one hour to your first receipt, at whatever level you start

This story has a hero, and it is not us. In March, Glacis Technologies published OVERT — an open, royalty-free standard for proof at the AI runtime boundary. It says what a runtime has to be able to prove, not just document: that a check ran, what it decided, which rules applied, and that a stranger can verify it without trusting anyone. Joe Braidwood and the Glacis team wrote it, published it under a patent covenant anyone can build on, and then did something rarer: when we shipped Beacon without saying where the ideas came from, Joe wrote to us plainly, and we fixed it in the open. Beacon implements OVERT. The Foundation does not issue standards; we implement this one, teach it, and carry it to the people the market will not reach — the nonprofit, the county, the clinic, the classroom, the student. The Fall 2026 Release is what carrying it looks like.
