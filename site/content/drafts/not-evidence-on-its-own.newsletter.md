Subject: Not evidence on its own — Horizon, and the receipt a stranger can check

Between 1999 and 2024 the UK Post Office used an accounting system called Horizon, built by Fujitsu. When Horizon's figures showed a shortfall, the Post Office treated the figures as proof and prosecuted the subpostmaster. More than 700 people were accused of theft or false accounting on the basis of system errors. There were prison sentences, bankruptcies and suicides across two decades. The reckoning came slowly and then all at once: a 2024 television drama, an Act of Parliament that quashed the convictions en masse, and a statutory inquiry that was still hearing evidence in 2025.
