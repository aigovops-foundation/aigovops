Privacy · plain language, no tricks

Your privacy

What this website collects, why, and the control you keep — in plain language, no fine print.

Draft for founder & legal review. A working draft for Bob and Ken to review with counsel before publishing. Written to be honest and accurate; not yet legal advice.

The AiGovOps Foundation builds careful, accountable technology — and we hold ourselves to the same care on the website you are using right now. This page covers www.aigovops-foundation.com. Our community platform and open-source tools each carry their own notices.

What we collect

  • Almost nothing, by default. You can read this entire site without an account and without telling us who you are.
  • Privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics. We use Plausible, which measures aggregate page views without cookies and without building a profile of you. It is not tied to your identity.
  • Your email — only if you choose to give it. If you subscribe to the newsletter, that happens on Substack under Substack's terms. If you email us, we of course see your message.
  • Anonymous, optional feedback. The "Was this page helpful?" control sends only your thumbs and any note you type — no account, and we ask you not to include personal information.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information. Not to advertisers. Not to anyone. You are a member of a community, never a product.
  • No surprise sharing. We won't hand your details to other companies or post your private information.
  • We never ask for your password, bank details, or money by message. If something claims to be us and asks for those, it isn't us.

Links out, and things we load

Pages link to our community platform, our open-source tools, our newsletter, and third-party sources. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Fonts and styling load from common web providers, which see only the standard technical request your browser makes to fetch a file.

Your information is yours

You are always in control. If you have subscribed or contacted us and want a copy of what we hold, or want it deleted, just ask — there is no catch. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, we honor it.

Questions

This site is run by the AiGovOps Foundation, a Washington State nonprofit. Email the stewards at bob.rapp@aigovops.community or ken.johnston@aigovops.community. A real person will read it. See also our Terms of Use.
